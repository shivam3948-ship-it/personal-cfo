# Interview prototype — notes

**Where:** Claude artifact "Personal CFO prototype" (design canvas, private; share it from the page before sending the link). 7 phone screens: get statement → upload (on-device) → x-ray → insight + show the math → ask your CFO → advice (prototype) → family view.
**Used in:** the T-3 interviews ([interview-kit.md](interview-kit.md), prototype tasks 1–6).

## Sample data (all fictional)
- **Rohan:** 10 funds, ₹26.36 L, 257 transactions since 2020, generated by `prototype-data/sample_portfolio.py` (synthetic market path incl. a 2022 drawdown, lump sums after rallies, a panic sale, a paused SIP). Investor XIRR 12.4% vs value-weighted fund return 13.2%.
- **Advice figures** from `prototype-data/advice_calcs.py`: FIFO lots, sample tax assumptions (12.5% LTCG above ₹1.25 L/yr, 20% STCG, 1% exit load < 1 yr), exemption shared across moves. Subtotals reconcile (asserted in the script).
- **Mr Sharma:** 6 regular-plan funds, ₹40 L; regular vs direct TERs are sample assumptions; extra cost ₹35,450/yr; 10-year corpus gap at a sample 10% gross return ≈ ₹7.54 L.
- **Overlap percentages** (78%, 61%) are sample inputs, not computed from real disclosures.

Outputs: `prototype-data/*.json`. Re-run: `python sample_portfolio.py && python advice_calcs.py` (the second reads `state.pkl` written by the first).

⚠ Sample tax rules are placeholders for the demo; production values come from `config/tax_rules.yaml` with sources (docs/12 §5).
