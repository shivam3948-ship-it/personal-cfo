# 32 · OKRs: Q4 2026 to Q1 2027

Targets marked ⚠ are proposals, to be reset once we have real baselines. Each key result says how it is measured.

## O1 · Prove that savers accept a voice answer at the money-back moment (Q4 2026)
- **KR1:** 10 interviews with first-time savers in Hindi, synthesised into the opportunity tree by 30 Nov.
- **KR2:** a concierge test with 15–20 savers. At least 60% of "where is my money" questions resolved without a ticket ⚠.
- **KR3:** 👍 on at least 70% of answers ⚠, and handoff to a person in at most one step in 100% of cases.

## O2 · Land one host-app pilot (Q4 2026 to Q1 2027)
- **KR1:** 5 partner conversations, at least 1 signed letter of intent by 31 Dec ⚠.
- **KR2:** a pilot A/B on the withdraw screen live by 28 Feb, with repeat support contacts per 1,000 savers measured in both arms.
- **KR3:** no drop in 30-day saving retention in the treatment arm (non-inferiority, with the margin agreed with the partner).

## O3 · Make it safe and cheap enough to run (Q1 2027)
- **KR1:** close S7–S9 from docs/28 (script CSP, shared rate limit, parsing on the device or via Account Aggregator) before any real user's data.
- **KR2:** cost per resolved conversation at or below ₹3 ⚠, measured from logs (estimate today: ₹1.65 per conversation, docs/34).
- **KR3:** a counsel memo on the advice boundary and the distributor vs RIA set-up (L-8) received.

## O4 · Turn the build into evidence for hiring (Q4 2026)
- **KR1:** a public write-up: research → opportunity tree → demo → results, with metrics.
- **KR2:** 3 wealthtech hiring conversations where the walkthrough is the topic.

**Not an OKR this period:** consumer launch, RIA registration, native apps (D-021, D-023).
