# 35 · Interview plan: first-time savers and the money-back moment (17 Oct 2026)

**Status:** the plan is ready, but **no interviews have been done yet.** This document can't replace them.

The existing kit (`docs/research/interview-kit.md`) covers Rohan, the investor. This plan covers Sunita, the first-time saver inside a host app (docs/24).

**Goal:** find out whether the money-back moment is the real pain, and whether a voice answer with a one-step human handoff is acceptable (the riskiest assumption, docs/30).

## Who (10 people)
- 6 who save small amounts in an app (daily or weekly), at least 3 of them outside the top 30 cities.
- 2 who stopped using a savings app after a bad withdrawal.
- 2 who save only through a chit fund, the post office or LIC (no app).

**Where to find them:**
- local shops and tailors (Sunita's world);
- family networks;
- app-store reviewers who wrote about withdrawals, but only by public reply on the store, never by tracking them down.

**Incentive:** ₹200 by UPI. ⚠ The amount is a judgement call, not a benchmark.

## Screener (WhatsApp, 1 minute, Hindi or English)
1. पिछले 3 महीने में आपने किसी ऐप में बचत की है? (हाँ / नहीं)
2. कितनी बार पैसे डालते हैं? (रोज़ / हफ़्ते में / महीने में)
3. कभी पैसे निकाले हैं? (हाँ, आसानी से / हाँ, दिक्कत हुई / नहीं)
4. फ़ोन पर ज़्यादा क्या करते हैं? (बोलकर / टाइप करके)

## Consent (read aloud, record the answer)
> "मैं एक छोटी रिसर्च कर रहा हूँ। आपका नाम, नंबर या खाते की जानकारी कहीं नहीं लिखूँगा। बातचीत की रिकॉर्डिंग सिर्फ़ नोट्स के लिए है और 30 दिन में मिटा दूँगा। कभी भी रुक सकते हैं। ठीक है?"

## Guide (30 minutes; past behaviour only, no pitching)
Questions are in the order to ask them.

1. **Last time money came back:** "Tell me about the last time you took money out. What happened, step by step?"
2. **The wait:** "While you waited, what did you do? Whom did you ask? What did you check?"
3. **The worst moment:** "Was there a moment you thought the money might be gone? What made you think that?"
4. **Who they asked:** "When something goes wrong with money, who do you ask first? Why them?"
5. **Talking vs typing:** "When did you last send a voice note about money? To whom?"
6. **Charges:** "Has money ever been taken that you didn't expect? What did you do?"
7. **Concept (last 8 minutes):** play the `/host?s=late` flow on a phone, without explaining it first.
   - "What would you do now?"
   - "Would you press 'हाँ' to talk to a person, or keep talking to this?"

Don't ask: "Would you use this?" or "Would you pay?" These questions invite polite yeses.

## Concierge test (2 weeks, after the interviews)
1. 15–20 savers send voice notes about their own savings to one WhatsApp number.
2. Shivam answers as Munim, using only what the saver's app shows (screenshots they share) and the host's published rules.
3. Log per case:
   - the question type;
   - time to answer;
   - resolved or not;
   - handoff asked for (yes or no);
   - 48 hours later: did the money arrive, and did they keep saving?

## Synthesis
- **Template:** one row per person.
  - Step where it hurt.
  - Feeling, in their own words.
  - Who they turned to.
  - Voice-note habit (yes or no).
  - Accepted a voice answer? Asked for a person?
- **Map onto the opportunity tree:** each finding confirms, kills or adds an opportunity.

## Pass and kill (set before starting)
| Signal | Pass | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| Withdrawal or debit doubt named unprompted as the worst moment | ≥ 6 of 10 | ≤ 3 of 10 |
| Would accept a voice answer if a person is one tap away | ≥ 6 of 10 | ≤ 3 of 10 |
| Concierge: resolved without a ticket | ≥ 60% | < 40% |
| Concierge: still saving 2 weeks later | No worse than before the test | Drops |

⚠ The thresholds are proposals; agree them before the first interview, not after.
