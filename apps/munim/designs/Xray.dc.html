Why yours can differ "Fund" is its own growth since your first purchase, the number factsheets advertise. "You" is your money's yearly return on your actual dates. SIPs buy more units when prices dip, so after a fall and a recovery they can beat the headline. Pausing SIPs in a fall, or buying big after a rally, usually puts investors behind their funds. {{d.discipline}}
